# Mobile Poker Rake Glossary

Terms used in mobile club poker listings and rakeback discussions. For the broader terminology collection, see the [Poker Catalog glossary](https://pppoker-catalog.com/glossary/).

## Rake

The fee removed from a pot or otherwise charged for running a game. A rake description normally combines a percentage with a cap. Mobile-club rules may vary by club, union, table, stake and format.

## Rake cap

The maximum rake charged for a hand. Caps may be expressed in big blinds, chips or currency and can change with table size or stake.

## Rakeback

A rebate based on rake attributed to a player. In this dataset, `max_rakeback_percent` is the highest rate advertised by the source listing; it is not a guaranteed individual rate.

## Dealt rake

A method that allocates an equal share of a hand's rake to every player who was dealt cards, whether or not the player voluntarily entered the pot.

## Contributed rake

A method that allocates rake according to how much a player contributed to the pot. Variants include weighted-contributed calculations.

## Weighted contributed

A contributed method in which a player's credited rake is proportional to that player's contribution to the raked pot.

## NFND (No Flop, No Drop)

A rule under which no rake is taken when a hand ends before the flop. The rule can be club- or table-specific; the dataset marks it only when the source explicitly displays NFND.

## Rake share

The portion of collected rake assigned to an agent or affiliate under a club arrangement. Player rakeback may be funded from this share.

## Chip guarantee

A claim or agreement that an agent will honor the value of chips credited through that agent. It is not the same as deposit insurance or a regulated banking guarantee.

## Traffic / connections

A reported measure of current or peak activity. Depending on the app or source, it may describe online players, seated players, connections or active tables; values from different sources are not always directly comparable.

## Union

A network of clubs that shares a player pool or tables. Commercial terms can still differ between clubs or representatives inside the same union.
